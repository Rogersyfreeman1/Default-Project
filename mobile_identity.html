📶 WiFi Security Tips

WPA3 - Best encryption. Your WiFi should use this.
WPA2 - Good, but WPA3 is better.
WEP/WPA - Outdated. Change your router settings.
Open WiFi - Never do banking on open WiFi. Use a VPN.
On your phone: Settings > WiFi > Tap your network > Check security type.
